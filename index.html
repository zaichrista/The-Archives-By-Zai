Profile

The perspective I bring.

My work starts with a question: why do certain spaces, sounds and objects make people feel like themselves? Growing up between cultures and moving between music, fashion and hospitality has shaped the way I notice those connections.

I studied Music at Oxford, where I learned to build an argument through close analysis. My writing explored how sound can create intimacy or disconnection, and how cultural experiences shape the way people understand themselves.

For my dissertation, I spent three weeks researching Tokyo’s underground music scene in Shibuya and Shimokitazawa. Through interviews, participant observation and fieldnotes, I examined how music, fashion and movement communicate belonging. The resulting 10,000-word study developed my ability to connect individual observations to a wider cultural argument.

At King’s College London, my MA in Cultural and Creative Industries brought that perspective into brand development. Bekaa, my 2026 academic project, proposes a Mediterranean-informed listening bar. It combines cultural and competitor research with positioning, financial planning and an experience built around music and repeat visits.

My practical experience includes designing and directing two runway collections, developing an independent modular fashion concept and supporting hospitality content production. Working as a stylist at Levi’s also gave me direct experience of customer preferences and the commercial decisions made on a shop floor.

At the Oxford Fashion Gala, I served as Co-Creative Director and Co-Marketing Director, connecting the show’s concept to campaign content and coordinating work across a volunteer team. At The Reach Brasserie, I have managed bar operations and developed a seasonal cocktail programme. I now lead the gradual implementation of its brand work across menus, signage, content and the guest experience. These roles keep my thinking connected to the people who have to deliver it.
